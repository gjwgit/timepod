TimePod Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Togaware Pty Ltd

TimePod does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind within the app. Nothing about when or how you use the app is sent to Togaware or to anyone else.

What TimePod stores

TimePod keeps only its display choices, and only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • What the clock shows, chosen under Display: the analog face, the digits, the date, the seconds, 24 or 12 hour time, and how the desktop clock looks and where it opens.
  • On a desktop, the size and position of the app window and the desktop clock, and whether the desktop clock was open, so the app reopens as you left it.
  • The light or dark theme and the page you were last on.

These are device preferences. They are never synced to a Pod or sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Logging in to a Solid Pod

Logging in is optional and the clock works fully without it. If you choose to log in, TimePod talks only to the Solid server you choose, and the login is kept on your device so you stay logged in. At this time TimePod reads and writes no data on your Pod. Future features that store data there, such as calendar events, will keep it encrypted on your own Pod, shared only if you choose, and this policy will be updated before they arrive.

Checking for a new version

When it starts, TimePod fetches its public change log, CHANGELOG.md, from GitHub, to show its version and whether a newer one is out. It is a plain download of a public file. TimePod sends nothing with it: no identifier, no settings and nothing about you. GitHub sees a request from your network, as it would from a web browser opening the same page, and that is covered by GitHub's privacy policy, not ours. This is the only connection TimePod makes on its own.

TimePod on the web

TimePod Web is the same app served to your browser. The web server delivers the app's files, and like any web server sees the requests for them. The app itself behaves exactly as described above, keeping its choices in your browser's storage for that site.

Children

TimePod is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date. TimePod is free software and its full history is public, so any change to what the app does is visible in the repository.
